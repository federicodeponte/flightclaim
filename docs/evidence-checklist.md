# Evidence checklist

> **Not legal advice.** Evidence requirements and acceptable formats vary by
> carrier, regulator, ADR body, and court.

Store claim evidence in a private encrypted location. Do not commit bookings,
identity documents, correspondence, payment details, or passenger information
to this repository.

## Core journey record

- Booking confirmation proving a confirmed reservation
- Original complete itinerary with scheduled departure and final-arrival times
- Ticket or fare breakdown when claiming downgrade reimbursement
- Proof identifying the operating carrier
- Evidence that directly connecting legs were on one reservation

## Disruption classification

### Cancellation

- Cancellation notice
- Timestamp and channel by which notice was received
- Re-routing offer with replacement departure and final-arrival times
- Evidence of whether the original flight operated

### Denied boarding

- Proof of timely presentation for check-in or boarding
- Valid travel-document evidence appropriate to the route
- Written refusal reason or contemporaneous notes
- Evidence that the booked flight operated or was intended to operate
- Evidence that surrender of the reservation was not voluntary

### Delay or missed connection

- Scheduled and actual departure times
- Scheduled and actual arrival at the final destination
- Door-opening time or an authoritative arrival record where available
- Re-routing record after a missed connection

### Downgrade

- Class originally purchased
- Class actually provided
- Fare attributable to the affected flight
- Itemised taxes and charges, identifying those unrelated to travel class

## Care expenses

- Itemised meal and refreshment receipts
- Accommodation invoice when an overnight or additional stay was necessary
- Airport-to-accommodation transport receipts
- Evidence that the carrier did not provide the required care
- A short note explaining why each expense was necessary and reasonable

## Correspondence and escalation

- Initial claim exactly as submitted
- Submission receipt or delivery proof
- Complete carrier response
- Full email thread headers where available
- Regulator or ADR form exactly as filed
- Every deadline source and calculation

## Privacy hygiene

- Redact documents only when the receiving body permits it.
- Send identity documents only through the body’s official channel when
  required.
- Remove document metadata before publishing examples.
- Never publish real booking references, identity numbers, addresses, contact
  details, payment data, or correspondence.
