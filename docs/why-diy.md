# Why consider DIY

> **Not legal advice or financial advice.** Compare current provider terms and
> the effort and risk of each route before assigning a claim.

Commercial claim agencies can be useful when a passenger does not want to
handle any correspondence or litigation risk. The trade-off is a contingency
fee deducted from a successful recovery.

Published fee models vary by provider, market, tax treatment, membership, and
whether legal action is used. A broad comparison range of roughly **25% to 50%**
of recovered fixed compensation captures common published models, but it is not
a quote. Verify the exact current terms, the fee base, VAT, legal-action uplift,
withdrawal terms, and treatment of direct airline payments before signing.

Examples of current provider pages:

- [AirHelp fees](https://www.airhelp.com/en-int/our-fees/)
- [Flightright FAQ](https://www.flightright.co.uk/faq)
- [Skycop price list](https://www.skycop.com/price-list-2/)

## Simple comparison

| Article 7 amount | 25% fee | 35% fee | 50% fee |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| EUR 250 | EUR 62.50 | EUR 87.50 | EUR 125 |
| EUR 400 | EUR 100 | EUR 140 | EUR 200 |
| EUR 600 | EUR 150 | EUR 210 | EUR 300 |

These examples apply a percentage only to the fixed Article 7 amount. Provider
contracts differ on what counts as recovered compensation.

## DIY advantages

- The passenger keeps the full recovery, excluding any direct filing or court
  costs.
- The passenger controls the factual classification, evidence, settlement, and
  escalation route.
- Carrier complaint channels, many enforcement processes, and eligible ECC-Net
  assistance are free.
- Article 9 expense claims can be documented directly without assigning a
  percentage to an intermediary.

## DIY costs and risks

- Time is needed to collect records, calculate timing, and follow up.
- National limitation, jurisdiction, and procedure can be difficult.
- Some enforcement bodies do not order individual payment.
- Litigation can involve filing fees, adverse-cost risk, service requirements,
  and language issues.
- A weak or incorrectly classified claim does not become stronger merely
  because it is self-filed.

## When an agency can be rational

An agency can be a reasonable choice when its current written terms are
acceptable and the passenger values avoiding the administrative or litigation
work more than the fee. Compare that option with a direct claim, a competent ADR
body, and
[free ECC-Net assistance](https://www.eccnet.eu/ecc-network/our-services)
where eligible.
